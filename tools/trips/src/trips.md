# Which trailhead do you use with your Enchantments permit?

Your permit zone decides where you can camp. Your number of nights does not change that rule. Core permit holders have two approaches. Eightmile/Caroline starts on a separate trail system. The other zones have their own camping restrictions.

## How many nights, at a glance

| Nights | What most parties did | Reports |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Camped outside the Core and went out the way they came in | 4 of 7 |
| 2 | One camp outside the Core both nights, a day hike into the Core | 7 of 9 |
| 3 | Colchuck Lake, a camp in the Core, then the Core or Nada Lake, out at Snow Lakes | 3 of 9. 7 of 9 camped in the Core |
| 4 or 5 | Colchuck Lake, then a new Core camp every night or two, out at Snow Lakes | 4 of 5 |
| 6 or 7 | No report found | 0 |

Each length has its own page: [one night](/trips/1-night/), [two nights](/trips/2-nights/), [three nights](/trips/3-nights/), [four to seven nights](/trips/4-to-7-nights/).

The hiker evidence here comes from 30 overnight reports dated 2013 to 2026, read September 28, 2026. Fourteen are Washington Trails Association reports. The sample leans toward Core trips. Fourteen parties camped there. Some writers did not state their permit zone, so their camps do not establish which permit they held.

## If you have a Core permit

**Agency rule.** Recreation.gov says “Core Enchantment permit holders may camp in any zone.” It expressly permits entry or exit by either the Snow Lake Trail or Stuart Lake/Colchuck Lake Trails. [Read the permit page](https://www.recreation.gov/permits/233273).

**Trailhead.** The agency describes approaches from Stuart Lake Trailhead and Snow Lakes Trailhead.

**Mapped distance.** Inspiration Lake is about 7.1 mapped miles from Stuart Lake Trailhead and about 10.8 from Snow Lakes Trailhead. These mapped lines read short.

**Hiker reports.** Nine Core-camping through trips started at Stuart Lake Trailhead. Six spent their first night at Colchuck Lake. Three crossed Aasgard Pass on their first day. [WTA writer AARshley](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/trip-reports/trip_report.2017-09-07.5978635091) describes one through trip.

## If you have a Colchuck permit

**Agency rule.** Recreation.gov permits day hiking in the Core but requires camping within the Colchuck Zone. It says camping immediately below Aasgard Pass is not allowed with this permit. [Read the zone text](https://www.recreation.gov/permits/233273).

**Trailhead.** The Forest Service names Stuart Lake Trailhead for the [Colchuck Lake Trail](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/okanogan-wenatchee/recreation/trails/colchuck-lake-trail).

**Mapped distance.** Colchuck Lake is about 4.0 mapped miles from that trailhead.

**Hiker reports.** Three Colchuck-camping trips entered the Core with day packs and returned the same day. [Skye Stoury's account](https://www.skyestoury.com/blog/colchuck-lake-backpacking) describes one.

## If you have a Snow permit

**Agency rule.** Recreation.gov allows day hiking in the Core but says you must camp at or below Upper Snow Lake. [Read the zone text](https://www.recreation.gov/permits/233273).

**Trailhead.** The agency approach is from Snow Lakes Trailhead.

**Mapped distance.** Snow Lakes are about 6.9 mapped miles from that trailhead.

**Hiker reports.** Four of six Snow-camping trips never crossed Aasgard Pass. [Moderately Adventurous](https://moderatelyadventurous.com/washington-enchantments-camping-and-permits/) describes a Snow Lakes camp with a Core day hike.

## If you have a Stuart permit

**Agency rule.** Recreation.gov permits day hiking in other zones but requires camping within the Stuart Zone. [Read the zone text](https://www.recreation.gov/permits/233273).

**Trailhead.** The [Stuart Lake Trail](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/okanogan-wenatchee/recreation/trails/stuart-lake-trail) begins at Stuart Lake Trailhead.

**Distance.** The Forest Service gives 4.5 miles to Stuart Lake. This project has no mapped line to that lake.

**Hiker reports.** [WTA writer Rhabbie](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/trip-reports/trip_report-2021-09-04-8475369096) reported about 12 hours from Lake Stuart into the Core and back, finishing in the dark. That is one party's time.

## If you have an Eightmile/Caroline permit

**Agency rule.** Recreation.gov restricts camping to Eightmile/Caroline. [Read the zone text](https://www.recreation.gov/permits/233273).

**Trailhead.** The Forest Service names [Eightmile Trailhead](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/okanogan-wenatchee/recreation/trails/eightmile-lake-trail).

**Mapped distance.** Eightmile Lake is about 3.6 mapped miles in. Lake Caroline is about 4.8.

**Hiker reports.** All three trips returned to Eightmile Trailhead. All visited Windy Pass. [Savvy Exploring](https://savvyexploring.com/post/northamerica/usa/eightmilelake/) describes one.

## Do you carry your pack up or down Aasgard Pass?

Fifteen reports describe carrying overnight packs over Aasgard Pass. Thirteen carried them up and three down. One did both. [WTA writer clandry](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/trip-reports/trip_report.2022-08-29.2880102745) and [Alec Sills-Trausch](https://explorewithalec.com/you-dont-need-to-thru-hike-the-enchantments/) disliked descending with packs. These accounts do not establish a direction for you. Neither agency names a traverse direction.

Recreation.gov's caution covers both directions. “Use extreme caution when climbing or descending steep snow slopes, exposed and hidden creeks and waterfalls are present.” [Read the full caution](https://www.recreation.gov/permits/233273).

## Eightmile/Caroline is separate from the traverse

The mapped Eightmile lines share no trail with the traverse. None of those three reported trips reached the Core. Check [the map](/map/) for the separate lines and mapped zone estimates. A zone edge on this map does not establish a campsite's status.

The free self-issued day-use permit, your overnight permit and your parking pass are three separate things.
