# You have two nights in the Enchantments

Eight of nine two-night reports kept one camp for both nights. Those parties used the middle day for a walk or climb. The ninth report does not say whether camp moved. Staying put was a pattern in these accounts, not an agency itinerary.

## What most parties did

Seven of the nine two-night trips camped at one lake outside the Core both nights. They spent the middle day on a day hike, often into the Core.

- **Snow Lakes side, 3 trips.** Day 1: Snow Lakes Trailhead to Upper Snow Lake (about 6.9 mapped miles). Day 2: day packs up into the Core. One reached Inspiration Lake. Day 3: out the same way.
- **Colchuck, 2 trips.** Day 1: Stuart Lake Trailhead to Colchuck Lake (about 4.0 mapped miles). Day 2: day packs up Aasgard Pass as far as Crystal Lake, or a day trip to Stuart Lake. Day 3: out the same way.
- **Stuart, 2 trips.** Day 1: Stuart Lake Trailhead to Lake Stuart (4.5 miles, per the Forest Service). Day 2: one party went up Aasgard and back, about 13 miles and 12 hours. The other went to Horseshoe Lake. Day 3: out the same way.
- **Core, 2 trips.** Day 1: Stuart Lake Trailhead, up Aasgard Pass with full packs, camp in the upper Core. Day 2: in the Core. Day 3: out to Snow Lakes Trailhead. One called it a long walk out.

The wider sample contains 30 overnight reports dated 2013 to 2026, read September 28, 2026. Fourteen are Washington Trails Association reports. The sample leans toward Core trips. Some writers did not state their permit zone. The sections below distinguish their camps from the agency's rules for your permit.

## If you have a Core permit

Both two-night Core-camping parties kept one camp in the basin. One used the intervening day for side trips. The other used it for a climb. [RobinB's Oregon Hikers account](https://www.oregonhikers.org/forum/viewtopic.php?t=27815) and [Little Grunts](https://www.littlegrunts.com/climbing-prusik-peak/) describe those trips.

Across the wider sample, hikers visited Little Annapurna and Prusik Pass. Gnome Tarn also appears in reports. These places are off the mapped traverse line. Their appearance in an account supplies no route instruction.

Recreation.gov says “Core Enchantment permit holders may camp in any zone.” It expressly permits Core entry or exit from either side. [Read the permit page](https://www.recreation.gov/permits/233273). Inspiration Lake is about 7.1 mapped miles from Stuart Lake Trailhead and about 10.8 from Snow Lakes Trailhead. The mapped lines read short.

## If you have a Colchuck permit

The two Colchuck-camping reports describe different middle days. [WTA writer aeflatley](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/trip-reports/trip_report.2014-06-18.2230375748) entered the Core and returned over Aasgard Pass with a day pack. [Mariah Adventures](https://mariahhadventures.com/2024/08/20/backpacking-the-enchantments-colchuck-lake-stuart-lake-permit-areas/) stayed outside the Core, visiting Stuart Lake and heading toward Horseshoe Lake.

Recreation.gov permits a Colchuck permit holder to day hike in the Core but requires camping within the Colchuck Zone. [Read the zone text](https://www.recreation.gov/permits/233273). The Forest Service names Stuart Lake Trailhead as the start of the [Colchuck approach](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/okanogan-wenatchee/recreation/trails/colchuck-lake-trail). Colchuck Lake is about 4.0 mapped miles from there.

## If you have a Snow permit

Three two-night reports camped in the Snow zone both nights and entered the Core on a day hike. Two name a Snow Lakes camp. The third says only the Snow zone. [WTA writer kreidykid](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/trip-reports/trip_report-2026-06-08.184711351531) reported reaching Inspiration Lake from an Upper Snow Lake camp. These are historical accounts, not information about today's trail.

Recreation.gov permits day hiking in the Core but requires a Snow permit holder to camp at or below Upper Snow Lake. [Read the zone text](https://www.recreation.gov/permits/233273). Snow Lakes are about 6.9 mapped miles from Snow Lakes Trailhead. Lake Viviane is about 9.6 mapped miles from the same trailhead. The Forest Service calls the trail beyond Upper Snow Lake “a primitive route marked with rock cairns.” [Read the trail page](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/okanogan-wenatchee/recreation/trails/snow-lakes-trail).

## If you have a Stuart permit

Two reports kept a camp at Stuart Lake. [WTA writer Rhabbie](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/trip-reports/trip_report-2021-09-04-8475369096) walked into the Core and back. The reported day took about 12 hours and ended in the dark. That is this party's experience, not an estimate for you.

In [retz.blog's account](https://retz.blog/posts/backpacking-at-lake-stuart-in-the-enchantments/), most of the group stayed outside the Core. Their day trips included Horseshoe Lake and Colchuck Lake.

Recreation.gov allows Stuart permit holders to day hike elsewhere but requires camping within the Stuart Zone. [Read the zone text](https://www.recreation.gov/permits/233273). The Forest Service gives 4.5 miles to Stuart Lake on its [trail page](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/okanogan-wenatchee/recreation/trails/stuart-lake-trail). That is the agency distance, not this project's mapped measurement.

## If you have an Eightmile/Caroline permit

No trip in this sample lasted two nights in Eightmile/Caroline. The three accounts there lasted one night. Two had planned to stay longer. All returned to Eightmile Trailhead and visited Windy Pass.

Recreation.gov restricts camping to the permit zone. [Read the zone text](https://www.recreation.gov/permits/233273). The mapped Eightmile lines are separate from the traverse. Lake Caroline is about 4.8 mapped miles from Eightmile Trailhead. See the [one-night accounts](/trips/1-night/) for that evidence.

## Before you use an account as a plan

Recreation.gov's caution for Aasgard Pass includes both directions. “Use extreme caution when climbing or descending steep snow slopes, exposed and hidden creeks and waterfalls are present.” [Read the full caution](https://www.recreation.gov/permits/233273).

The free self-issued day-use permit, your overnight permit and your parking pass are three separate things.
