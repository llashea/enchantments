# You have one night in the Enchantments

Seven trips in this sample lasted one night. Three went into Eightmile/Caroline. One camped at Colchuck Lake and entered the Core for a day hike. Three walked from one trailhead to the other. In all three through trips, the second day continued into the evening.

## The usual trip, day by day

**Camping at Colchuck Lake, from Stuart Lake Trailhead.** Fits a Colchuck or Core permit. One report did this.

| Day | Route | Mapped miles | Night |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stuart Lake Trailhead to Colchuck Lake, about 2,200 ft up. In the afternoon, day packs up Aasgard Pass and back | 4.0, then 3.1 round trip | Colchuck Lake |
| 2 | Colchuck Lake to Stuart Lake Trailhead | 4.0 | Home |

**Camping at Lake Caroline, from Eightmile Trailhead.** Fits an Eightmile/Caroline permit. Three reports, two at Lake Caroline and one at Eightmile Lake.

| Day | Route | Mapped miles | Night |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Eightmile Trailhead to Lake Caroline. In the afternoon, up to Windy Pass and back | 4.8, then 5.0 round trip | Lake Caroline |
| 2 | Lake Caroline to Eightmile Trailhead | 4.8 | Home |

Three more reports went trailhead to trailhead in one night. All three ran long. One made an unplanned camp at the foot of Aasgard and finished near 10 p.m. One reached the Colchuck boulders at dusk. One took far longer than planned coming down from Lake Viviane.

## If you have a Core permit

**Your permit.** Camp in any zone and start or finish at either trailhead.

**Suggested plan.** Cross the whole traverse, with one night in the Core.

| Day | What to do | Mapped miles | Sleep at |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stuart Lake Trailhead to Colchuck Lake, then up Aasgard Pass with full packs into the Core | 7.1 to Inspiration Lake | Inspiration Lake |
| 2 | Through the Core and down past the Snow Lakes to Snow Lakes Trailhead | 10.8 | Home |

Another way: camp at Colchuck Lake and day hike up Aasgard instead. It is a much shorter weekend. All three one-night crossings in the reports ran into the evening or later.


## If you have a Colchuck permit

**Your permit.** Every night at Colchuck Lake or elsewhere in the Colchuck Zone. Day hikes into the Core are allowed. You cannot camp right below Aasgard Pass.

**Suggested plan.** Camp at Colchuck Lake. Take day packs up Aasgard Pass the first afternoon.

| Day | What to do | Mapped miles | Sleep at |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stuart Lake Trailhead to Colchuck Lake, about 2,200 ft up. Afternoon: day packs to the top of Aasgard Pass, about 2,300 ft up, and back | 4.0, then 3.1 round trip | Colchuck Lake |
| 2 | Colchuck Lake to Stuart Lake Trailhead | 4.0 | Home |

Another way: skip the pass and walk the shore of Colchuck Lake. Aasgard is steep loose rock and often snow.

Trip reports: [Skye Stoury's one-night account](https://www.skyestoury.com/blog/colchuck-lake-backpacking).

## If you have a Snow permit

**Your permit.** Every night at or below Upper Snow Lake. Day hikes into the Core are allowed.

**Suggested plan.** Camp at Upper Snow Lake. Walk up to Lake Viviane in the evening or the next morning.

| Day | What to do | Mapped miles | Sleep at |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Snow Lakes Trailhead to Upper Snow Lake | 6.9 | Upper Snow Lake |
| 2 | Morning: day packs up to Lake Viviane and back. Then Snow Lakes to Snow Lakes Trailhead | 5.2 round trip, then 6.9 | Home |

Another way: camp at Nada Lake, 5.8 miles in, and keep both days short.

Trip reports: [WTA writer Abhigdeal](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/trip-reports/trip_report-2020-08-02-2875732823).

## If you have a Stuart permit

**Your permit.** Every night in the Stuart Zone, around Lake Stuart. Day hikes into other zones are allowed.

**Suggested plan.** Camp at Lake Stuart. Day hike to Horseshoe Lake or Colchuck Lake before you walk out.

| Day | What to do | Mapped miles | Sleep at |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stuart Lake Trailhead to Lake Stuart | 4.5, per the Forest Service | Lake Stuart |
| 2 | Morning day hike to Horseshoe Lake or Colchuck Lake, then out to Stuart Lake Trailhead | Not on the app's map | Home |

Lake Stuart is not on the app's mapped line, so the side trips have no mapped miles.


## If you have an Eightmile/Caroline permit

**Your permit.** Every night in the Eightmile/Caroline Zone. It is a separate trail system from the traverse.

**Suggested plan.** Camp at Lake Caroline. Walk up to Windy Pass the first afternoon.

| Day | What to do | Mapped miles | Sleep at |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Eightmile Trailhead to Lake Caroline. Afternoon: up to Windy Pass and back | 4.8, then 5.0 round trip | Lake Caroline |
| 2 | Lake Caroline to Eightmile Trailhead | 4.8 | Home |

Another way: camp at Eightmile Lake, 3.6 miles in, for a shorter first day.

Trip reports: [Savvy Exploring](https://savvyexploring.com/post/northamerica/usa/eightmilelake/), [zilcsak.com](https://zilcsak.com/blog/enchantments-eightmile-caroline-zone-backpacking).

## Before you go

The agencies do not name a traverse direction. Recreation.gov's Aasgard Pass caution covers climbing and descending. “Use extreme caution when climbing or descending steep snow slopes, exposed and hidden creeks and waterfalls are present.” [Read the full caution](https://www.recreation.gov/permits/233273).

The free self-issued day-use permit, your overnight permit and your parking pass are three separate things. Entry must be on your permit's stated date. [Read the permit rules](https://www.recreation.gov/permits/233273).
