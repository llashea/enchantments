An app for the Enchantments Traverse

Offline map and permit-zone companion for the Stuart Lake → Aasgard Pass → Snow Lakes route in Washington's Alpine Lakes Wilderness. Permits come only from Recreation.gov — see the facts below.

What the app shows

Built for the Enchantments. Works offline.

The map, permit facts and your saved plan live on the phone. Airplane mode is the normal state.

GPS position

See your position along the mapped route, with distance ticks in miles or kilometres. GPS works in airplane mode.

Your permit zone

Save the zone you won and the map emphasizes it. Zone outlines are mapped estimates — good for which zone, not for a boundary line.

The lakes, in order

Colchuck to Snow Lakes, named in walking order with heights. Mapped water only — nothing here claims what's flowing today.

Terrain that reads

Shaded relief, contour lines and an optional satellite view, with the route drawn by height.

Trip recording

Record your traverse in the background and export a GPX. Tracks stay on the phone unless you share them.

Honest limits

Permit facts carry dates and sources. Anything unverified is labelled or absent — never invented.

Compared with general apps

Made specifically for the Enchantments.

General hiking apps have offline maps and GPS. Enchantments Traverse Planner adds:

  • The five permit zones on the map, with your planned zone emphasized
  • Permit season, lottery and day-use facts with as-of dates
  • Route-position ticks along the mapped line
  • Trailhead, parking and getting-there logistics
  • Plain statements of what the data can and cannot claim

Support Privacy Terms Data & attribution Official permits
The Enchantment Permit Area is managed by the Okanogan-Wenatchee National Forest. This app is independent — not affiliated with or endorsed by the US Forest Service or Recreation.gov, and it never applies for, checks or holds a permit. Overnight permits come only from Recreation.gov. Route length and heights are mapped and modeled estimates, not surveys. Check current conditions and closures before you go.
