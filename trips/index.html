Up or down Aasgard Pass with a full pack

Most people carry their packs up it. Of 15 trip reports that took overnight packs over the pass, 13 went up and 3 went down, and one did both. That is why the Core plans start at Stuart Lake Trailhead and finish at Snow Lakes Trailhead. Two writers who went down with packs said going down was the worse way. Neither agency names a direction.

It is about 2,300 ft up from Colchuck Lake in about 1.5 mapped miles. Recreation.gov's caution covers both directions. “Use extreme caution when climbing or descending steep snow slopes, exposed and hidden creeks and waterfalls are present.” Read the full caution.

Getting from one trailhead to the other

The Core plans end at a different trailhead from where they start. Snow Lakes Trailhead and Stuart Lake Trailhead are both off Icicle Road, about 4.3 and 12.5 miles from Leavenworth. Plan a second car or a shuttle. Road closures and the drive in are on the roads page.

Before you go

These plans are built from 30 overnight trip reports dated 2013 to 2026, read September 28, 2026, and the app's mapped line. Most of the reports are Core trips. The Forest Service says “Camp only at previously impacted sites.” Read the permit rules.

The free self-issued day-use permit, your overnight permit and your parking pass are three separate things. Zone edges on the map are mapped estimates. See packing and the app, which has the map offline.
